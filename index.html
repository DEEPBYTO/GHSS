About Us

Welcome to GHSS Tibba Sultan Pur

Government Higher Secondary School Tibba Sultan Pur is dedicated to providing quality education to students from the region. Our institution has a rich history of academic excellence and community service.

We offer a comprehensive curriculum that balances academics, sports, and extracurricular activities, preparing students for higher education and successful careers.

Our dedicated faculty and staff work tirelessly to create a supportive learning environment where every student can thrive and reach their full potential.
