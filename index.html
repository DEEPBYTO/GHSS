About Our School

Education for Knowledge, Character and Progress

Government Higher Secondary School, Tibba Sultan Pur, is dedicated to supporting students in their educational journey and preparing them for future opportunities.

Our goal is to encourage learning, responsibility, discipline and personal development in a supportive educational environment.

We believe education plays an important role in developing capable individuals and contributing to society.
